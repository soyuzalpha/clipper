# Product Requirements

## Product Vision

An AI-powered operating system for content creators and clippers.

The system connects the entire content lifecycle:

Discover
→ Understand
→ Strategize
→ Ideate
→ Plan
→ Produce
→ Publish
→ Measure
→ Learn
→ Repeat

# AI Clip Pipeline

Long-term pipeline:

1. Ingest video
2. Extract audio
3. Transcribe
4. Detect interesting segments
5. Score segments
6. Generate clips
7. Generate subtitles
8. Generate hook/title
9. Generate caption
10. Generate hashtags
11. Format for 9:16 platforms

- TikTok
- Instagram Reels
- YouTube Shorts

# Viral Potential

Never claim guaranteed virality.

Score based on:

- hook strength
- curiosity
- emotional intensity
- novelty
- pacing
- retention potential
- shareability
- audience relevance
- controversy
- clarity
- storytelling

Output:

Viral Potential Score

With breakdown:

- Hook
- Curiosity
- Emotional Impact
- Shareability
- Retention Potential

Also explain:

- Why this may work
- What could hurt performance
